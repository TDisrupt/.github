## About TDisrupt

[TDisrupt](https://tdisrupt.com/) is an independent technology publication focused on artificial intelligence, startups, blockchain and real-world assets, digital infrastructure, enterprise technology and emerging technologies.

We publish technology news, analysis, research, explainers and industry perspectives for founders, executives, investors, engineers and technology professionals.

Our editorial focus is on understanding what changed, why it matters and how emerging technologies, companies and infrastructure are shaping businesses and the wider digital economy.
